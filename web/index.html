0.15×

A 50-leg accumulator is paid at 0.15 of the advertised odds.

That multiplier is published by SportyBet at /api/ng/factsCenter/betType/config. It starts at 0.95 for two or three legs and falls to 0.15 at 47 legs and above.

Figures below were verified against the live API on 19–20 November 2026. This page is static. Live fetch and a slip calculator arrive in Phase 1.

Two other published facts belong on the same screen, once Phase 1 ships:

  • A 1.25× “odds boost” maps to an RTP band of 0.00–0.50 at /api/ng/marketing/v1/activities/oddsBoost/getOddsBoostLobRtpRatio. A boosted selection can pay nothing.
  • All 21 games in the lobby return rtp: null. The field exists. It is not filled. Base market overround runs 7–9%.

SportyAgent does not pick winners. It makes the cost of a slip visible. Run this page locally with ./start.sh. It binds 127.0.0.1:3000 only.
